## The claim

One sentence. What is true that the reader might not already believe?

## For whom

Who is this for, and what are they trying to do when they need this?

## The story

The evidence, example, or experience that makes the claim land. Keep it concrete.

## Evidence

Facts, numbers, quotes, or reproducible steps that back the story. Cut fluff.

## What to take away

What should the reader do or notice differently after this?
