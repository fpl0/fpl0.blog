# Ideas

Weekly capture of one-line seeds. Claim only — not essays, not outlines.

Not part of the Astro content collection. Kept at repo root on purpose so it never
publishes.

## Angle sources

When stuck, pull from:

- **Work scars** — something that cost you time, money, or trust
- **Strong disagreement** — a common take you think is wrong
- **Explain-once** — a thing you keep re-explaining to different people
- **Before/after** — what changed when you (or someone) did X
- **Tiny craft** — a small technique worth naming so others can reuse it
